# Returns

You can return most items within 30 days of delivery for a full refund to the original payment method. Items must be unused and in their original packaging. Refunds are issued within 5 business days after we receive the parcel.

# Return shipping

Return labels are free for faulty or damaged items. For change-of-mind returns a flat 6 dollar return label fee is deducted from the refund.

# Delivery times

Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 business days. Express delivery takes 1 to 2 business days and costs 9 dollars. Orders placed before 2 pm on a business day ship the same day.

# Damaged or missing items

If an item arrives damaged or is missing from your parcel, tell us within 7 days of delivery and we will send a replacement or refund at no cost.

# Changing or cancelling an order

An order can be changed or cancelled while its status is processing. Once it has shipped it cannot be cancelled, but you can return it after delivery.

# Gift cards

Gift cards never expire and can be used on any product. They cannot be exchanged for cash.

# Payment methods

We accept major credit and debit cards, and digital wallets. We never store full card numbers.

# Warranty

All furniture and ceramics carry a 12 month warranty against manufacturing defects. Normal wear and accidental damage are not covered.
